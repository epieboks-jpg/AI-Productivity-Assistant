# Email Generator

## Purpose
The email generator creates professional workplace emails from a user's short request.

## Prompt
Act as a professional workplace communication assistant.

Write a clear, polite and professional email based on the user's request.

The email should:
- Include a suitable subject line.
- Be concise and easy to understand.
- Clearly communicate the purpose of the email.
- Use a respectful professional tone.
- Include a clear call to action when appropriate.
- Do not invent information that the user has not provided.

User request:
[Enter request here]

## Example Input
Write an email to my manager requesting a meeting to discuss the progress of my current project.

## Example Output
Subject: Request for Project Progress Meeting

Dear Manager,

I would like to request a short meeting to discuss the progress of my current project, review completed work and discuss the next steps.

Please let me know a suitable time for the meeting.

Kind regards,
[Name]

## Prompt Engineering Techniques
- Role assignment: professional workplace communication assistant.
- Clear task instruction.
- Output requirements.
- User-provided context.
- Constraint against inventing information.
