# Research Assistant

## Purpose
The research assistant helps users structure workplace research and organise information.

## Prompt
Act as a professional research assistant.

Help me research the topic below.

Provide:
1. A clear definition of the topic.
2. Key questions to investigate.
3. Important themes or areas to research.
4. A suggested research structure.
5. A list of information that should be verified.
6. Suggested types of reliable sources.

Clearly distinguish between established information and information that requires verification.

Do not invent sources, statistics or quotations.

Research topic:
[Enter topic here]

## Example Input
How can artificial intelligence improve workplace productivity?

## Example Output
### Definition
Artificial intelligence refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as generating text, analysing information and assisting with decisions.

### Key Questions
- Which workplace tasks can AI automate?
- How can AI reduce repetitive administrative work?
- What are the risks of using AI at work?
- What skills do employees need to use AI effectively?

### Research Areas
- AI automation
- Employee productivity
- Prompt engineering
- Data privacy
- Accuracy and human oversight
- AI ethics

### Information to Verify
Statistics about productivity improvements, specific company results and claims about AI performance should be verified using reliable sources.

## Prompt Engineering Techniques
- Defined research role.
- Clear output structure.
- Verification requirement.
- Explicit instruction not to fabricate sources or statistics.
