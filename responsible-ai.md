# Responsible and Ethical AI

Responsible AI is an important part of the AI-Powered Workplace Productivity Assistant.

## 1. Privacy
Users should avoid entering confidential company information, passwords, financial information, personal information or other sensitive data into AI tools unless the organisation has approved the tool and its data practices.

## 2. Accuracy
AI can generate incorrect information. Important emails, research findings, summaries and recommendations should be reviewed by a human before use.

## 3. Human Oversight
The assistant supports employees but should not replace appropriate human judgement, approval or accountability.

## 4. Bias
AI outputs may reflect bias in training data or in the information supplied by users. Outputs should be reviewed for unfair or inappropriate assumptions.

## 5. Transparency
Users should understand when AI has been used to generate or transform content, particularly where workplace policies require disclosure.

## 6. Security
AI tools should be used according to organisational cybersecurity and information-management policies.

## 7. Appropriate Use
The assistant is intended for productivity support. It should not be treated as an unquestionable authority or as a replacement for qualified professional advice.

## Responsible AI Checklist
- [ ] No unnecessary confidential information was entered.
- [ ] AI-generated information was reviewed.
- [ ] Important facts were verified.
- [ ] Human approval was obtained where required.
- [ ] Organisational AI policies were followed.
